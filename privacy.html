ServicesServ Backup · Personal-use app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Purpose and Google Drive access

ServicesServ Backup is a self-managed application for its owner's backups. It uses rclone and Google Drive OAuth to access files in the Google Drive account explicitly authorized by the owner, for backup upload, listing, checking, retention and restoration.

What data is processed

The integration can process backup files and Google Drive file metadata necessary for those operations. Backup payloads are encrypted using Restic before upload. Google OAuth access tokens are held by the owner's configured backup environment, not by this public website.

Sharing and storage

The application does not sell personal data or distribute backup contents to unrelated parties. Encrypted backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive, subject to Google's storage and privacy policies. This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not receive backup contents or OAuth credentials.

Website analytics and cookies

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts or site-set cookies. GitHub, as the website host, may process technical access information under its own privacy policy.

Control and deletion

The owner controls the backed-up files and their retention, can delete them from Google Drive, and can revoke OAuth access in their Google Account settings. The backup encryption password is not published on this website.

Contact

Questions about this personal-use integration can be submitted through the project's GitHub Issues page.

Кратко на русском

Приложение предназначено только для резервного копирования личного сервера владельца. Доступ к Google Drive предоставляется владельцем через OAuth. Резервные копии шифруются Restic до загрузки. Публичный сайт не получает файлы резервных копий, токены или пароли.

Владелец управляет хранением и удалением файлов в Google Drive и может отозвать OAuth-доступ в настройках Google-аккаунта. На сайте не установлены сторонняя аналитика или реклама.
