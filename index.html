ServicesServ Backup

A personal, owner-operated backup integration that uses rclone to access the owner's Google Drive and Restic to create encrypted backups.

What the app does

The integration is used by its owner to upload, list, verify, retain and, when necessary, restore encrypted backup files in their own Google Drive account. It is not a publicly offered backup service.

Backup contents are encrypted by Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Access to the Drive account is granted by the owner through Google's OAuth authorization flow and may be revoked in the Google account's security settings.

О приложении

ServicesServ Backup — личная интеграция для резервного копирования. Restic шифрует резервные копии перед отправкой, а rclone обеспечивает доступ к Google Drive владельца через OAuth.

Интеграция не предоставляется как публичный сервис. Доступ к данным контролирует владелец аккаунта Google.
